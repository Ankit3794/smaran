Three design directions: every screen, same flow

Each direction covers the full v1 flow: connect your AI, share from X, the save confirmation, the home screen, topics, a single topic's timeline, the detail view, search, waiting and failed states, and settings. There's also one later screen showing reminder notifications. The content is identical across all three so you can compare the look and feel directly. The names Smaran, Trove and Glean are placeholders for now; we'll pick the real name separately.

The same in all three: Share → the save is stored instantly (works offline) → the AI fills in a one-line summary, topic and tags in the background → you browse by topic + time or search by keyword → each card shows a thumbnail, a one-line summary and the source → tapping opens the original. A small dot marks saves you've never opened; later, reminders will use this.
